# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Curious visitors are the primary audience. They need to understand the result before exploring the search details.
Researchers can inspect the candidate data and completed experiments.

## Product purpose

Explain what the search attacks, what it finds, and how the evidence supports each result.
Keep the live run separate from completed research.

## Brand personality

Calm, precise, candid. Preserve the existing dark research interface.

## Anti-references

Avoid unexplained terminal output, flashing alerts, and scores without context.
Avoid treating readable text as proof of a solution.

## Design principles

- Explain the current result in plain language at the top.
- Keep the active search chain and Jev signal in two columns immediately below.
- Fit both live panels above the fold on desktop screens.
- Place candidate results and their verification status below the live panels.
- Distinguish the current chain from the candidate pool.
- Define measurements beside the data.
- Expose full candidate text for inspection.
- Keep completed experiments separate from live measurements.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use readable contrast, semantic controls, keyboard navigation, and visible focus.
Support narrow screens and reduced motion. State meaning in text as well as color.

## Confirmed direction

The user selected a calm, precise interface with readable text, keyboard access, and reduced motion.
Keep both live panels above the fold. Introduce technical details after the plain-language explanation.
